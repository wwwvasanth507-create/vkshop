What happens when you approve or cancel an order?

Approve & Confirm moves the order to Confirmed so the seller can ship it. For UPI, the payment is marked Completed and earnings/rewards are credited now. For Cash on Delivery, earnings/rewards were already credited at checkout, and the payment itself stays Pending until it's collected on delivery.

Cancel Order cancels the order, restores product stock (and, for Cash on Delivery, reverses any earnings/rewards already credited), and the customer receives a notification: "Your payment details is incorrect. So, the order is cancelled by the payment verifiers. If any trouble please make complaint with appropriate proofs. Thank You!"

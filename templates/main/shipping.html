Shipping Rates & Policies

Information about delivery speeds, courier partners, and shipping charges.

At VKshop, we coordinate all item dispatches through VKshop Logistics to ensure your packages are handled with care and delivered on time. We partner with leading courier providers to support secure tracking links for every order.

1. Shipping Costs

Our shipping rates are calculated dynamically based on your order subtotal:

Order Total Range Standard Shipping Fee
Under INR 500.00 INR 50.00
INR 500.00 and above FREE Shipping

2. Delivery Timelines

Timelines depend on the location of the seller store and the customer shipping address:

  • Metro Cities: 2 to 4 business days.
  • Non-Metro / Tier-2 Cities: 3 to 6 business days.
  • North-East & Rural Areas: 5 to 8 business days.

3. Order Tracking

Once an order is confirmed and packaged by the seller, a unique logistics tracking number (e.g. TRK-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX) is generated. You can check the current tracking status inside your account dashboard under the order details view.

4. Damaged or Lost Shipments

If a package arrives damaged or does not arrive within the expected timeline, please file a complaint ticket directly through your customer portal, or contact us at support@vkshop.com.

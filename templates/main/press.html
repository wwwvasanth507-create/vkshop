July 13, 2026

VKshop Completes System-Wide Brand Transition

VKshop (formerly known as E-COM) today announced the completion of its comprehensive corporate rebranding strategy. The brand update features an updated high-resolution logo icon, simplified frontend layout navigation, and a modern design system.

"Our new branding reflects our status as a premium storefront and verified marketplace," said the VKshop Leadership Team. "It represents trust, high quality, and seamless logistics as we continue to scale."

June 18, 2026

VKshop Logistics Launches Real-Time Dispatch Verification

In our continuous effort to eliminate shipping delays and coordinate dispatch pipelines, VKshop Logistics has rolled out printable CID order delivery sheets and barcode verification checks. Store owners can now easily batch-print up to 4 shipping cards per A4 page to coordinate packaging and courier tracking numbers dynamically.

May 05, 2026

VKshop Implements Advanced KYC Fraud Safeguards

To provide customers with verified marketplace transactions, VKshop has added Aadhaar document checks and onboarding signature verifications for all store registers. The system automatically restricts store activations until background verifiers approve the profile, ensuring total transaction security.

Returns & Replacements Policy

Information about eligibility, timelines, and how to request a return or replacement.

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you receive a product that is defective, damaged, or does not match the description on VKshop, you can easily request a return or replacement.

1. Return Window

Most products purchased on VKshop are eligible for returns or replacements within 7 days of successful delivery. The exact window may vary based on specific categories or seller profiles.

2. Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a refund or product replacement:

  • The item must be unused, unwashed, and in the same condition as received.
  • The product must contain original brand tags, pricing labels, packaging boxes, and warranty cards intact.
  • Serial numbers or barcodes on the product must match the transaction invoices generated by VKshop.

3. How to File a Return Request

You can initiate a return directly from your dashboard:

  1. Go to your Customer Dashboard and navigate to the Orders list.
  2. Select the order details and click on Request Return.
  3. Specify whether you require a full refund or a replacement item, select the reason code, and upload a proof image (if the product is damaged or defective).
  4. Once the merchant or admin verifiers approve the request, a courier partner will schedule a pickup.

4. Refund Settlements

Refunds are processed back to the original payment source (credit card, debit card, bank transfer, or wallet deduction) within 5 to 7 business days after the returned product passes quality checks at the seller's store.

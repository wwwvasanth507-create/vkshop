Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 13, 2026

At VKshop, accessible from our online marketplace, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by VKshop and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our service to you:

  • Personal Data: While using our service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you, including your name, shipping address, billing address, phone number, and email.
  • Usage Data: We collect data regarding how the service is accessed and used, including search queries, page views, items clicked, and session cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

VKshop uses the collected data for various purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our premium marketplace.
  • To process your transactions and coordinate shipping with VKshop Logistics.
  • To verify identity documents (such as Aadhaar files and signatures) for seller registration.
  • To monitor usage patterns and enhance application security.

3. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, deletion of, or modification of the personal data we hold about you. You can update your account details directly inside your customer dashboard.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@vkshop.com.
